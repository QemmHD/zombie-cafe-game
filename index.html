🧟 How to run your Zombie Cafe

  • Goal: Run a cafe staffed by zombies. Cook spooky dishes, serve human customers for 🪙 coins, and grow your undead empire.
  • Stoves auto-cook their set recipe. Click a stove to choose what it cooks (better dishes unlock as you level up).
  • Each customer orders a specific dish (shown in their bubble) from what your appliances are cooking. Watch for ⭐ VIPs (pay double), patient kids, and impatient bikers.
  • 🧟 Staff opens your zombie roster — each zombie has a name, levels up as it serves (getting faster), and can be fed there.
  • Direct your zombies: tap a zombie to select it, then tap a stove to make it tend the cooker (cooks much faster), or tap a customer to send it to serve them. Tap it again to feed it.
  • 🤖 Auto toggles automatic serving — leave it on for a relaxed game, or turn it off to manage every zombie by hand like the original.
  • Serving and tending drain a zombie's energy — when it runs out they collapse and rest 💤.
  • Feed zombies by clicking them (uses 🥩 flesh) to restore energy fast.
  • Build tables, appliances and decorations from the bar below the cafe. Decorations raise appeal, bringing customers faster.
  • Appliances: the 🍳 Stove makes drinks & soups, the 🔥 Grill makes hot food, the 🥧 Oven bakes pizza & cake. Each only cooks its own dishes.
  • 🧱 Expand renovates the cafe, knocking out walls to unlock more floor space (costs coins + toxin).
  • 🧪 Infect a seated customer to turn them into a brand-new zombie staffer (costs toxin).
  • ⚔️ Raid the city with free zombies to bring back coins, flesh and toxin.
  • ✋ Edit lets you pick up furniture to move it, or sell it back for coins.
  • 🎯 Goals track objectives — claim coin, toxin and flesh rewards as you hit them.
  • ⭐ VIP customers (with a crown) pay double and tip well — serve them fast!
  • Tap a waiting customer to bump them to the front of the serving queue.
  • Your cafe auto-saves, and keeps earning coins while you're away.
