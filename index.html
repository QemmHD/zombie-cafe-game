🧟 How to run your Zombie Cafe

  • Goal: Run a cafe staffed by zombies. Cook spooky dishes, serve human customers for 🪙 coins, and grow your undead empire.
  • Stoves auto-cook their set recipe. Click a stove to choose what it cooks (better dishes unlock as you level up).
  • Zombies automatically carry finished food to seated customers. Serving drains their energy — when it runs out they collapse and rest 💤.
  • Feed zombies by clicking them (uses 🥩 flesh) to restore energy fast.
  • Build tables, stoves and decorations from the bar below the cafe. Decorations raise appeal, bringing customers faster.
  • 🧪 Infect a seated customer to turn them into a brand-new zombie staffer (costs toxin).
  • ⚔️ Raid the city with free zombies to bring back coins, flesh and toxin.
  • Your cafe auto-saves to this browser. Use 💾 to save anytime.
